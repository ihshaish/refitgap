Analysis plan: shallower repairs of the final sequential model on replay's final buffer (BiGRU)
==============================================================================================

Aim
---
This plan tests whether the final buffer of the paired replay run, used after sequential
training to repair the final sequential model, can substitute for replay during training at the
final evaluation point. We
compare four repairs of increasing depth with replay, at the endpoint only.

Design
------
- BiGRU with frozen GloVe embeddings on ASRS, periods numbered 0 to 7: the vocabulary stream
  (principal), the reweighted stream (secondary) and their two control streams. Seeds 0 to 9 on
  4 streams give 40 cells, paired by seed. The stored sequential and replay runs (5% reservoir)
  of the method comparison are reused. Sequential training is rerun only to save checkpoints and
  must reproduce its stored run exactly; otherwise that seed stops on that stream, with no retry
  and no replacement. With fewer than 6 passing seeds a stream is not tested. No interim look.
- Four repairs under one shared rule, each started from the final sequential checkpoint:
  (1) threshold refit only, which uses no buffer record; (2) logit adapter, a full 9 x 9 affine
  map on the label logits; (3) head, the classifier layer on the penultimate features; (4) full
  model, every trainable parameter. Repairs 2 to 4 are fitted on the final buffer of the same
  seed's replay run (3342 records on the vocabulary pair, 731 on the reweighted pair) with
  Adam, batch 64, an optional penalty towards the start, fixed epochs and no evaluation data.
- Union of buffers (secondary, vocabulary stream only): repairs 2 to 4 fitted on the union of all
  eight recorded buffers (about 9300 records), with the final-buffer recipe, step count and
  period shares. It is an expanded-data comparison, not one at equal memory, and is not tuned.
- Recipes: chosen per intervention stream and repair on held-out tuning seeds 100 and 101, which
  withhold 5% of each period's training pool; never on seeds 0 to 9 or on test scores. Grid:
  learning rate {1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2} x penalty {0, 1} x epochs {1, 3, 10, 30}. Selected: the
  highest held-out historical score among settings whose held-out current score is at least the
  threshold refit's minus 0.0025; ties at three decimals go to fewer steps, then the smaller
  learning rate, then the larger penalty. If none qualifies, the highest current score is taken
  and flagged. A grid-edge setting is reported and the grid is not extended.

Analysis
--------
- Shared rule: one threshold per label for each arm, fitted on the pooled fitting halves of
  the test sets of periods 0 to 7 and scored by macro-F1 on the held-out halves, as a mean over
  200 partitions and both directions (50 in tuning). Historical is the mean over periods 0 to 6
  and current is period 7. The calibration records (about 1040 per period on the vocabulary
  pair, 915 on the reweighted pair) lie outside the buffer and are the same for every arm.
- Contrasts per seed: historical, repair minus replay (principal); current, repair minus
  sequential and repair minus replay; recovery, repair minus sequential on historical periods;
  for the union of buffers also union minus final buffer. Margin 0.005 on the historical periods
  and on the current period (against sequential and against replay), alpha 0.05.
- Tests: non-inferiority by a one-sided paired t test against -0.005, Holm-adjusted within its
  family, together with a one-sided sign-flip test; inferiority by the mirror pair of tests, for
  a contrast that is also material and negative. Also reported: 95% paired t intervals, TOST at
  +/-0.005 (the only basis for "equivalent"), the two-sided t test and BCa intervals. Classes:
  equivalent; material (Holm-adjusted t p < 0.05 and a mean of at least 0.005 in size); small
  non-zero; unresolved, which also covers a two-sided sign-flip p of 0.05 or more.
- Test families, Holm over the four levels of repair within each, per intervention stream:
  historical (four); current against sequential (three, the threshold refit being zero by
  construction); current against replay (four); recovery (three, two-sided only). No repair is
  designated in advance. A repair is named adequate by an intersection-union test: the largest
  of its three non-inferiority p-values, Holm across the four repairs, every sign-flip condition
  met. The union of buffers has five families of three: historical, the two current contrasts,
  and union minus final buffer on historical and on current.
- Cost condition on training passes, required for "low-cost": the repair's full-network passes
  on a seed, plus its share of the recipe search (the grid's passes on the stream's two tuning
  runs divided by the 10 seeds), are below replay's extra training examples (about 70000 on the
  vocabulary stream) on every passing seed.
- Secondary, deciding nothing: the reweighted stream; contrasts differenced against the control
  stream; both arms scored on the control environment; inherited thresholds, thresholds refitted
  on first-period validation data, per-period rules and macro-AUPRC; replay minus sequential
  under the shared rule and at the intermediate checkpoints 1 to 6.

Predictions
-----------
1. A low-cost repair reaches replay without unacceptable current-period loss (named adequate,
   cost condition met): the final buffer supports an adequate endpoint alternative to replay.
2. Replay retains a material advantage (historical contrast material and negative for all four
   repairs). This would not show that historical content must be present throughout training.
3. Historical recovery comes with current-period deterioration (recovery material and positive,
   current against sequential material and negative): a recovery-adaptation trade-off.
4. Differences remain unresolved: the study does not decide substitutability.
5. "The tested repairs did not substitute for replay" is written only if every repair is
   inferior on the historical contrast or on current against replay.
6. Union of buffers: the final buffer already suffices; or the union improves on it by a
   material amount and reaches replay, so access to discarded records helps; or neither reaches
   replay; or unresolved.
7. Expectation: the threshold refit fails historical non-inferiority on the vocabulary stream,
   since most of that stream's deficit is attainable gap (0.194 of 0.219) and a threshold refit
   moves only the mismatch. No direction was stated for the other three repairs.

Date
----
29 October 2026, before any run of this design and before any of its outcomes was computed. The
recipes were tuned on 29 October, after the plan and before any repair on seeds 0 to 9. Seeds 0
to 9 had been analysed in the method comparison and the shared-rule analysis of the same runs
had been read, so replay's lead and the threshold refit's result were known and item 7 was no
longer open. We report the results as an extension on reused seeds.

Departures
----------
- On 30 October 2026, before any outcome was computed, all 24 reruns of seeds 0 to 5 on cloud
  CPUs failed to reproduce their stored runs, while the 16 reruns of seeds 6 to 9 passed. We set
  the no-retry rule aside once and reran seeds 0 to 5 on the processor model inferred for the
  stored runs. Only exactly reproducing models are analysed.
- Five of the six selected recipes lie on a grid edge; the full-model recipe on the vocabulary
  stream (learning rate 1e-3, no penalty, 3 epochs) does not.
