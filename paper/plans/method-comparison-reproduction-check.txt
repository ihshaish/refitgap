Method comparison: the reproduction check
=========================================

Check
    The sequential-training runs made again for the method comparison on the CPU cluster had to
    reproduce the runs stored in August: identical inherited thresholds and a largest
    elementwise score difference of at most 1e-3 on every cluster seed. Runs on a local machine
    were to be reported only.

Outcome
    4 of 40 runs fell outside the tolerance, with identical thresholds on all four: reweighted
    stream seed 2, 0.0029; its control seed 2, 0.0044; control seed 5, 0.0034; control seed 3,
    0.244. On the last one, macro-F1 at the inherited thresholds differs from the stored run by
    at most 0.0038.

Response
    We waived the check in a note written before any method contrast was computed or looked
    at. The other checks ran first and passed (the stored decomposition reproduced within
    0.0002 per cell, against a tolerance of 0.002). No tolerance was widened, no run was dropped
    or replaced, and the analysis is unchanged: all 40 runs are kept and the comparison is
    reported as a new paired cohort. A repeat of control seed 3 on a fifth node matches the run
    used in the comparison bit for bit, so run-to-run randomness does not explain the difference;
    node-dependent arithmetic remains a guess.
