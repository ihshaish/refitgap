Analysis plan: training regimes
===============================

Aim
---
This plan tests whether the composition of backward degradation under sequential training, its
split into an attainable gap and a change in mismatch, changes when the input embeddings adapt
more strongly, when each period receives more optimisation, or both. The first factor is
plasticity of the input embeddings only (the GRU and the head train at every level); the second
is optimisation per period under this learner, not a general statement about under-training.

Design
------
ASRS primary stream, BiGRU with GloVe embeddings, sequential training. Two factors, with levels
taken from earlier work only:
  lambda, the multiplier on the embedding learning rate: 0, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0;
  E, epochs per period: 3 (the setting of every primary result) and 10.
Primary cells: the corners lambda in {0, 1.0} x E in {3, 10}, seeds 0 to 9, 40 runs.
Secondary cells (dose curve, descriptive): lambda in {0.1, 0.3} x E in {3, 10}, seeds 0 to 2, 12
runs. All 52 runs are new and seeded, so the same ten seeds appear in every primary cell. The 12
runs of the earlier 3-seed sweep are not reused; they are prior information only.

Measures
--------
From stored scores, with exact F1 optimisation over all cutpoints, split-half cross-fitted over
200 partitions, on historical periods j = 1..N-2 (the primary averaging range). Per cell and
seed:
  BWT_att    attainable backward transfer: the mean over periods of the final model's attainable
             level minus that of the period's own model. Primary outcome.
  BWT_op     the mismatch part of backward transfer: the final model's macro-F1 at inherited
             thresholds minus its attainable level, minus the same for the period's own model.
  BWT_total  macro-F1 backward transfer at inherited thresholds, = BWT_att + BWT_op.
Absolute components are primary. No conventional-range average and no recovery ratio is used.

Tests
-----
Central: the interaction
  Delta_int = [BWT_att(lambda=1, E=10) - BWT_att(lambda=0, E=10)]
              - [BWT_att(lambda=1, E=3) - BWT_att(lambda=0, E=3)],
on seed-paired differences, with a 95% percentile interval from a paired bootstrap over seeds
(20000 draws).
Secondary, reported and deciding nothing:
1. The effect of lambda on BWT_att at E = 3 and at E = 10 (seed-paired corner contrasts, bootstrap
   intervals).
2. The effect of E on BWT_att at lambda = 0 and at lambda = 1.
3. Within each E, the monotonic trend of BWT_att over the four lambda levels: Spearman rho with an
   exact permutation test blocked within seed (Pearson recorded).
4. The same contrasts for BWT_op and BWT_total.

Expectations
------------
1. BWT_att becomes more negative with lambda and with E.
2. BWT_op moves the opposite way, and BWT_total is comparatively flat at E = 3.
3. No expectation for the sign of Delta_int.
Precision: the earlier sweep's between-seed standard deviation of the attainable component is
about 0.003 to 0.004. With 10 paired seeds a corner contrast has a standard error of about 0.0013
to 0.0018, so effects of the size already seen (about 0.007 between lambda = 0 and lambda = 1)
would have an interval excluding zero. Smaller effects are reported with their intervals and not
turned into null statements.
Commitments: all cells are reported, with no selection and no levels added or removed afterwards;
the central contrast does not change after any cell is seen; a failed run is rerun with the same
seed, and a cell missing after two attempts is reported missing, with no seed substitution. The
result enters the paper as one boundary subsection with one figure and a full table in the
appendix, never as a test of representation forgetting in general. Whatever the outcome, the
paper's central statement stands: in one regime the deficit is mostly mismatch, and an attainable
loss in another regime shows why the decomposition is needed.
