Forward comparison by lag
=========================

Aim
---
A stale model, trained through period i, can be scored on a later period j beside the
up-to-date model. By lag, this plan splits the stale model's shortfall into attainable gap and
mismatch. Five further analyses use the same stored scores.

Data
----
Stored scores only; no training. The ASRS stream under the two frozen-embedding conditions
(GloVe governs), 10 seeds each; the benchmark streams; the MultiEURLEX paired refit runs where
stored in the same format. A stream without stored forward cells is reported missing, never
substituted. Macro-F1 on the 0 to 1 scale, 200 split-half partitions.

Analysis
--------
1. Forward decomposition by lag. For periods j = 2 to 8 and lags 1 to 7, both models are scored
   on period j's test records. Shortfall of the stale model: up-to-date minus stale at the
   inherited thresholds. Attainable part: the same difference in cross-fitted attainable level
   (shared partitions). Mismatch part: the remainder. Means over the cells of a lag, then over
   seeds, with 95% percentile bootstrap intervals over seeds (20000 resamples, seed 20260920).
   The mismatch share is formed only where the shortfall's interval
   excludes zero. Governing contrast, on ASRS with GloVe: the share at lag 1 against the mean
   share over lags 3 to 6, with the interval of the difference, and the same contrast on the
   mismatch part itself; a lag with no formed share counts as no difference shown. Beside it: a
   balanced profile (lags 1 to 4 on the last four periods only) and a secondary profile under
   the stale model's own-period rule. Lag 7 has one cell. The lag-0 cells (zero by definition)
   and the backward cells are compared with the paper's values. Other streams are read after
   ASRS.
2. Per-label account on ASRS, periods 2 to 7, backward decomposition, both conditions together
   (20 observations). The label means must reproduce 0.0125, 0.0012 and 0.0113 to within 5e-5.
   All nine per-label changes in mismatch are given with intervals, and
   the share of Ground Event / Encounter (78% in-sample in the paper).
3. Curvature as a predictor of per-label mismatch, on all 64 cells of ASRS with GloVe. Measured
   mismatch m: F1 at the in-sample best threshold minus F1 at the inherited threshold.
   (a) Check of the theorem: p1 = half the curvature of the smoothed F1 curve at its maximum
   times the squared threshold displacement (201 thresholds, Gaussian width 0.02; 0.01 and 0.04
   as sensitivity). Rank and linear correlation of p1 with m, the slope of m on p1 (near 1
   expected only at displacements up to 0.05; bins 0.05, 0.10, 0.20), and the correlation of m
   with the squared displacement alone. No p-values. The paper's displacement correlation, 0.46
   to 0.72, is the baseline.
   (b) A predictor without labels: p2 = half a curvature proxy (density of scores within 0.05
   of the inherited threshold; 0.025 and 0.10 as sensitivity) times the squared change in mean
   score between the validation split and period j, with one fitted scale. The scale is fitted
   leaving one label out; a split by period is secondary. An earlier test of the prevalence slope
   alone was weak; this is the second test and is reported as such.
4. Inherited thresholds refitted with the exact optimiser in place of the grid: deficit,
   attainable gap, change in mismatch and levels on periods 2 to 7, beside the printed values.
   Direction two-sided in advance.
5. Margin sensitivity. Every planned outcome word of the paper's contrasts is recomputed at
   margins 0.003, 0.004, 0.005, 0.0075 and 0.010, with the same test families and Holm
   structure. The 0.005 column must reproduce the printed outcomes. One table, a row per
   contrast and a column per margin.
6. Calibration subsample. The shared rule is refitted on calibration subsamples of the buffer's
   size (3342 on the vocabulary stream, 1801 on MultiEURLEX), 200 draws, both arms on the same
   draw: repair minus replay on the historical periods and the current period, mean and 95% t
   interval over seeds. Run only where per-record scores are stored; otherwise we report what
   is missing.

Predictions
-----------
1. Check of the theorem (3a): rank correlation of p1 with m of at least 0.6.
2. Predictor without labels (3b), read on ASRS with GloVe only: pooled held-out rank
   correlation of at least 0.4, and a predicted mismatch share within 0.15 of the measured one.
   Both are reported whichever way they fall.
The lag profile carries a governing contrast but no predicted direction. The prediction that
the attainable part is at least 0.8 of the shortfall at every lag from 1 to 5, for the
fine-tuned DistilBERT, belongs to the DistilBERT paired refit plan.

Departures
----------

was read: the bootstrap resamples in 1, first written as 2000; the tolerance on the label means
in 2, first written as 1e-6; and the scale in 3(b), first written as fitted on seeds 0 to 4 and
tested on seeds 5 to 9 with a fixed slope of -1.14.
