Analysis plan: repeated constructions of both drifts
====================================================

Aim
---
The paper's constructed streams were each built once. This plan tests whether the two
compositions (a vocabulary change gives mostly an attainable gap, a prior tilt gives mostly
mismatch) hold when the construction itself is drawn again.

Starting point
--------------
Two vocabulary constructions, each realised once. Fixed pairing: the nine labels are paired
once, the top 200 mutual-information tokens per label (document count at least 50, first period
only) are exchanged rank for rank between paired labels, and each occurrence is exchanged with
probability rho from the fourth period onward (875 tokens, 34% of running text). Per-period
permutation: the pooled top 400 tokens per label (document count at least 40; 1697 tokens, 84%
of running text) are permuted anew in each period from the third onward, each occurrence
rewritten with probability rho. Published attainable shares of the deficit (10 seeds, against
the control): 0.836 [0.746, 0.937] for the fixed pairing at rho = 0.5 and 0.887 [0.871, 0.905]
for the permutation at rho = 1.0; the two rows of the reweighted stream have upper interval
limits of 0.14.

Design
------
Learner throughout: BiGRU on frozen GloVe embeddings, sequential training, 3 epochs per period.
First design (21 September 2026): 3 new pairings at rho = 1.0 and 0.5 and 3 new permutation
draws at rho = 1.0, construction seeds 20260921 to 20260923, model seeds 0 to 2, 27 runs,
against the primary-stream control.
Second design (11 October 2026): the same three construction seeds extended to the prior tilt at
rho = 1.0 with its uniform control (keep 0.35, tilt 5, onset in the fourth period); model seeds
0 to 2; 45 runs.
Final design (11 October 2026, later), which replaces both run lists. Primary: the pair matched
in size in the paper, the strong reweighting (0.037 = 0.001 + 0.036) against the half-dose
pairing (0.034 = 0.028 + 0.006), with nearly equal deficits that decompose in opposite
directions. Construction seeds 20260921 to 20260928 (8); per seed, the full prior tilt with its
uniform control and the half-dose pairing (rho = 0.5) with the primary stream as control; model
seeds 0 and 1; 48 runs. Secondary: the full-dose pairing and the permutation at rho = 1.0 for
construction seeds 20260921 to 20260923, model seeds 0 and 1; 12 runs. All 60 runs are reported.

Analysis
--------
The final model is rescored with thresholds fitted to its own scores on first-period validation
data, and attainable levels come from exact split-half selection over 200 partitions, on all
periods. Against the control matched by seed: D = U(control) - U(arm), A = V(control) - V(arm),
R = D - A, and the shares A/D and R/D. In the final design D, A and R are means over a
construction's 2 model seeds, and the unit of inference is the construction (8): the mean over
constructions of R - A for the prior tilt and of A - R for the pairing, with a percentile
bootstrap over the 8 constructions and the count with the predicted sign.

Predictions
-----------
Vocabulary (first design; in the final design they apply to the secondary runs):
1. For every mapping the attainable share A/D exceeds 0.75 at rho = 1.0 and 0.60 at rho = 0.5.
2. Every mapping's attainable share exceeds 0.14, the upper limit of both rows of the reweighted
   stream.
3. Falsifier: any mapping at rho = 1.0 with an attainable share below 0.50. The result would
   then depend on which vocabulary was moved and would be narrowed to the realisations run.
4. Descriptive: the between-mapping standard deviation of the attainable share at rho = 1.0,
   beside the between-seed standard deviation (0.0019 on A over 10 seeds).
Prior tilt (second design):
5. For every tilt draw the mismatch share R/D at rho = 1.0 exceeds 0.80 and A/D is below 0.15.
6. Falsifier: any tilt draw with A/D above 0.30, which would mean the attainable term depends on
   which records the tilt drew.
7. Descriptive: the between-draw standard deviation of A/D and of R/D.
Matched pair (final design, primary):
8. For every construction the prior tilt gives R - A > 0 and the half-dose pairing gives
   A - R > 0, scored as the count out of 8 with the predicted sign, for each mechanism.
9. The construction-level means of R - A for the prior tilt and of A - R for the pairing both
   have bootstrap intervals over the 8 constructions that exclude zero.
10. Falsifier: any construction in which the sign flips (a prior tilt with A > R, or a half-dose
    pairing with R > A).
11. Descriptive: between-construction standard deviations of A, R, A/D and R/D for each
    mechanism, beside the between-seed values of the published rows, and the deficits D of the
    two mechanisms per construction, to show that the match in size holds.
No estimator, control, label set, period, learner or dose level changes, and there is no
selection among constructions. The published rows stay as printed.

Date
----
21 September 2026, before any stream of the design was built or any model trained on one; the
two parts of 11 October 2026 before any of their runs. Only the streams of construction seed
20260921 had been built by then, to check the builder.

Departures
----------
The first two run lists were not run as written; the final design replaced them before any run.
Predictions 1 to 4 are therefore scored on 2 model seeds per construction, not 3.
