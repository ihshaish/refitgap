Analysis plan: carried rules against the refitted lead, from stored scores
=========================================================================
Fixed: 19 November 2026
Nothing below was computed before this date.

Status
------
Every analysis here was designed after the results of ASRS, MultiEURLEX and PubMed were known.
All are descriptive: estimates, intervals and counts by sign, without outcome classes. None
replaces a result of Table C.1 or of the PubMed plan. No training, no new stream, period, label
or initialisation. A small estimate is not read as equivalence, and the refitted lead is the
comparison at a stated label budget, not a true lead against which another rule is wrong.

Aim
---
To show, for one comparison on three streams, how far the lead under each carried rule lies from
the lead after both models refit; to test how far the ASRS result depends on the sample of
records and on single labels; and to add one stored comparison of LwF with replay.

Inputs
------
Final models of the paired DistilBERT runs, scores on the test records of every period, labels,
inherited thresholds, and the final model's scores on the first-period validation split:
  ASRS         primary_distilbert_{naive,replay}_s0-5 (distilbert-paired-refit.txt)          6 pairs
  MultiEURLEX  eurlex_distilbert_{naive,replay,replaycur}_s0-5 (distilbert-paired-refit.txt,
               distilbert-matched-updates.txt)                                               6 pairs
  PubMed       pubmed_distilbert_{naive,replay,replaycur}_s0-7 (pubmed-paired-refit.txt)     8 blocks
The ASRS and MultiEURLEX files hold no record identifiers. Their row order is rebuilt from the
stream file, whose hash matches each run and whose test labels equal the stored labels in order;
this is stated wherever those rows are used. BiGRU on MultiEURLEX: the sequential and
replay runs in results/multieurlex_bigru/ and the LwF runs of the method comparison
(eurlex_C2_lwf_a0.1_T2_lam1_s0-9), 10 blocks.
Before any new quantity: the code reproduces the DistilBERT rows of Tables C.1 and C.2 and the
three PubMed comparisons to 4 decimals. A failure stops the analysis until resolved.

1. Carried rules for replay minus sequential training (DistilBERT, periods 2 to 7)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per pair and period, on the 200 partitions of the paired refit (default_rng(7000+100*seed+period)),
both arms are scored on each scoring half under:
  a. the inherited thresholds theta1 of each arm;
  b. a cut of 0.5;
  c. own validation: each final model's thresholds fitted on the first-period validation split
     with that model's scores, as defined for Table C.2;
  d. the previous period's rule: thresholds fitted for each final model on 500 test records of
     period j-1, drawn without replacement by default_rng(11000+100*seed+period), one draw per
     partition, the same records for both arms;
  f. a matched rate of positive predictions: per label and scoring half, the share of records
     each arm flags at theta1 is averaged over the two arms, and each arm flags that share of
     records, those with its highest scores (ceil(share * n) records, ties by record order).
     The cuts need no labels;
  e. the refit on the opposite half of the target period (the paired refit), and the same refit
     with 500 records per fitting half (default_rng(9000+100*seed+period), as in the PubMed plan).
For each carried rule r in a to d and f: the lead G_r, and the difference G_r - G_V computed within the
pair on the same scoring halves, averaged over halves, partitions and periods, then over pairs.
Rule d is set against the 500-record refit, the others against the full refit; both differences
are printed for d. Each with a 95% paired t interval and the count of pairs by sign. Each arm's
level under every rule is printed beside the leads, since an unchanged lead can hide a loss in
both arms. The whole-period value of G_r is given beside the same-fold value for a to c.
Rule d is a rule carried from the previous period, not a refit; it is not a test of what a
deployment could have known, since the final model was trained through the whole stream.
The same table is computed for replay minus extra updates on current data on MultiEURLEX and
PubMed, for the supplement.

2. Dependence of the ASRS result on records and labels
------------------------------------------------------
a. Records and initialisations resampled together. 1000 replicates. In each: the 6 pairs are
   drawn with replacement; for each period 2 to 7 and each of 10 partitions of the original
   records into halves, records are drawn with replacement within each half (so no record is in
   both a fitting and a scoring half), one draw shared by both arms and all pairs; thresholds are
   refitted within the replicate. Percentile 95% intervals for G_U (same folds), G_V and the
   change, printed beside the published intervals over initialisations, which remain the paper's
   inference. Seed default_rng(20261118). The uncertainty is over records of this stream, not
   over other corpora.
   Also run for replay minus extra updates on current data on MultiEURLEX.
b. Labels. For each of the 9 labels: test positives per period, and the label's part of G_U, of
   G_V and of the change (parts sum to the macro values, checked before rounding), with paired
   intervals; the three macro values with each label left out in turn; for the two labels with
   the largest part of the change, false positives and false negatives of each arm at theta1 and
   at the refitted thresholds, per 1000 test records of the period, on the scoring halves.
   The same table for MultiEURLEX; PubMed's is already in its analysis output.

3. LwF against replay on MultiEURLEX (BiGRU, stored values)
-----------------------------------------------------------
The ten blocks share the first-period model, theta1, labels and partitions (checked).
Per block: replay's lead over LwF at the inherited thresholds, after both refit, and the change;
means with percentile bootstrap intervals as for the other BiGRU rows (20000 resamples,
default_rng(20260920)) and counts by sign. Reported as a change in replay's lead over LwF.
LwF's weight (0.1) was carried over from ASRS and not tuned on this stream.

4. Constructed streams (run only if the files allow)
----------------------------------------------------
Condition: for the reweighted stream, the vocabulary stream and their two controls, stored
scores of the final sequential and replay BiGRU models on the test records of periods 2 to 7,
with labels and theta1, for the same ten initialisations. If any is missing the item is dropped
and the reason recorded.
One contrast per manipulation: the change in replay's lead on refitting (G_V - G_U, Table C.1
protocol) on the manipulated stream minus the same change on its control, paired by
initialisation; mean, bootstrap interval, counts by sign. The reweighted stream and its control
hold different records, so that contrast also contains the change in the evaluation records;
the vocabulary stream and its control hold the same records with altered text. No statement
about the cause of the natural streams' results rests on it.

Left out
--------
- Evaluation prevalence of one label varied at fixed scores.
- The forward comparison of Appendix H stays where it is; it changes both the model and the
  period scored and is not a row of item 1.

What each outcome changes in the manuscript
-------------------------------------------
Item 1  A compact main-text panel (three streams, rules a to d and f against the refit) whatever it
        shows. If the previous period's rule reads as the refit does, the comparison is said to
        need less than labels from the scored period, on these records; if not, the dependence
        on the source of labels is stated in Section 5.1 and the limits.
Item 2  If the interval over records and initialisations keeps the refitted ASRS lead inside
        the margin, one sentence in Section 5.1; if it does not, the ASRS sentence in the
        abstract and Section 5.1 is weakened to match, and the published test is still reported.
        If one label carries most of the change, that is stated with its counts.
Item 3  One row in Table C.1 and one sentence.
Item 4  One sentence joining Sections 5.1 and 5.2 and a supplement row, or nothing.

Outputs
-------
One CSV per item at the level of stream, pair, period, arm, rule, source of fitting records,
number of fitting records, partition, label, with scoring records, positives, tp, fp, fn, F1;
one JSON of summaries; the scripts; a log of the reproduction check. Tables and the panel are
built from the same summaries.

Compute
-------
CPU only. Items 1, 2b, 3 and 4 are minutes. Item 2a is about 1.4 million threshold fits per
stream; it runs on a larger CPU machine if it would take more than a few minutes on a desktop.
