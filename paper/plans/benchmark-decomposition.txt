Analysis plan: the decomposition B = A + Delta R on the four benchmark streams
=========================================================================

Aim
---
The paper decomposed the backward deficit at inherited thresholds on the ASRS primary stream
only. On the four benchmark streams it reported that thresholded and threshold-free backward
transfer diverge, but no scores were kept, so the attainable gap A and the change in mismatch
Delta R could not be formed. This plan measures the composition of the deficit on these streams.
The study is observational and does not extend the controlled result.

Design
------
Streams: HuffPost, Amazon, arXiv cs subcategories and arXiv primary archives, as built.
Learner: BiGRU on frozen GloVe embeddings, 3 epochs per period, sequential training, unchanged.
Seeds 0 to 4, those of the published benchmark rows: 20 runs. Every period model is scored on
every period's test set and the scores are kept with the inherited thresholds theta1.

Analysis
--------
From the stored scores: macro-F1 of the final model and of the period's own model at the run's
theta1; their attainable levels by exact split-half selection over 200 partitions shared between
the two models; then the deficit B, the attainable gap A, the change in mismatch Delta R and the two
mismatches, per run and period. Means over periods 2 to 7 and over all historical periods 1 to
7. Percentile bootstrap over the 5 seed blocks, 20000 resamples.
The new runs' macro-F1 and macro-AUPRC cells are compared with the published rows of the same
stream and seed. Training is not bit-deterministic, so the agreement is descriptive.

Predictions
-----------
The paper read the benchmark figure as follows: on arXiv cs subcategories, Amazon and HuffPost
the thresholded decline lies below the threshold-free one; on arXiv archives it lies above.
1. On each of arXiv cs subcategories, Amazon and HuffPost, Delta R is positive on periods 2 to 7
   (mean over seeds).
2. On the same three streams Delta R is the larger part of the deficit, Delta R > A.
3. On HuffPost, where both measures fall, A is positive and larger than on the ASRS primary
   stream (A > 0.0012).
4. On arXiv archives Delta R is smaller than on each of the other three streams.
5. Falsifier: if on any of the three streams of prediction 1 the attainable gap is the larger
   part (A > Delta R), the reading that the thresholded excess is mismatch does not hold outside
   ASRS as a pattern, and the paper names the exception.
6. Descriptive, no threshold: A/B and Delta R/B per stream, with the ASRS value (Delta R/B = 0.90)
   as the reference row, and the per-period profile of B, A and Delta R.
No stream, learner, threshold procedure, estimator or period changes. All 20 runs are reported
whatever they show.
