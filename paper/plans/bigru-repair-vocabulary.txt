Analysis plan: full-model repair against replay on the vocabulary stream (BiGRU, new seeds)
==========================================================================================

Aim
---
On new training runs, this plan tests whether full-model repair of the final sequential model,
fitted on the final buffer of the paired replay run, falls more than 0.005 below replay on
historical performance under the shared rule. The shallower repairs had found it inferior on
seeds 0 to 9 (-0.0185, interval -0.0221 to -0.0149), on seeds that had been analysed before. New
seeds give new training runs within the same setting: corpus, stream construction, learner and
the test and calibration records are those of the shallower repairs.

Design
------
- Learner and streams: BiGRU with frozen GloVe embeddings on the ASRS vocabulary stream
  (principal) and its control stream, periods numbered 0 to 7. The reweighted pair is not used.
- Arms: sequential training, replay (5% reservoir) and extra updates on current data (as many
  extra current-period records as replay's buffer records, the same number of steps).
- Seeds 200 to 207, eight seeds, all on one processor model on cloud CPUs. No seed is added
  after looking and a failed seed is not replaced. Results are not pooled with seeds 0 to 9.
- Repair: full model only, on both streams, started from the final sequential checkpoint and
  fitted on the final buffer of the same seed's replay run (3342 records on the vocabulary
  stream). The recipe is that of the shallower repairs, unchanged and not retuned: learning rate
  1e-3, no penalty, 3 epochs (159 steps), Adam, batch 64, no evaluation data. No logit adapter,
  no union of buffers.

Analysis
--------
- Shared rule, as in the shallower repairs: one threshold per label for each arm, fitted on
  the pooled fitting halves of the test sets of periods 0 to 7, macro-F1 on the held-out halves,
  mean over 200 partitions and both directions. Historical is the mean over periods 0 to 6 and
  current is period 7. The calibration records (about 8300) lie outside the buffer.
- Contrasts per seed, on the vocabulary stream and not control-adjusted: historical, repair
  minus replay (the only primary); current, repair minus sequential and repair minus replay;
  recovery, repair minus sequential on the historical periods.
- Margin 0.005 on the historical periods and on the current period, alpha 0.05, at least 6
  passing seeds. Each contrast is a test family of one, so there is no Holm adjustment.
- Inferior: the two-sided t test and two-sided sign-flip test both below 0.05 with a mean of at
  least 0.005 in size, and the one-sided t test and one-sided sign-flip test beyond -0.005 both
  below 0.05. Non-inferior: the one-sided t test and one-sided sign-flip test against -0.005
  both below 0.05. Otherwise unresolved. Recovery is tested two-sided only.
- Adequate: non-inferior on the historical contrast and on both current contrasts
  (intersection-union), and the cost condition on training passes: the repair's full-network
  passes are below replay's extra training examples in every passing seed (10026 against 70182
  on seeds 0 to 9). The cost of the recipe search (1203120 passes) is reported beside it and is
  not part of the condition, unlike in the shallower repairs.
- Secondary family of three, Holm over the three for the t and TOST p-values: (a) replay minus
  extra updates on current data, historical, under the shared rule; (b) replay's reduction of
  the control-differenced shared-rule cost, on periods 1 to 6; (c) replay minus extra updates on
  the control-differenced attainable gap of the method comparison, on periods 0 to 7.
- Descriptive: control-differenced historical contrast, recovery as a share of the sequential
  deficit, arm levels under the inherited and refitted thresholds, macro-AUPRC, replay's lead at
  checkpoints 1 to 6 and at first learning of each period, and the costs.

Predictions
-----------
1. Adequate (as defined above): the final buffer supports an adequate endpoint alternative to
   replay; this would be contrary to the result on seeds 0 to 9.
2. Inferior on the historical contrast: the repair did not substitute for replay, which confirms
   the earlier full-model result in the same setting only. It would not show that later repair
   is impossible, or that historical content must be present throughout training.
3. Trade-off: recovery material and positive while current performance is below sequential by
   more than 0.005.
4. Otherwise unresolved: the new-seed study does not decide substitutability.
"Confirmed on new seeds" is written only when the planned test rejects in the planned
direction; "not confirmed" says nothing about the opposite; "contrary" only when the opposite
test rejects. A current-period shortfall against sequential never reads "did not substitute".
5. Expected values: the repair inferior, with a mean of about -0.010 to -0.025; (a) large and
   positive (+0.1285 on seeds 0 to 9); (b) near +0.02 (+0.0220); (c) near +0.0829. The spread
   may be larger than on seeds 0 to 9.
6. Planning power for inferiority with eight seeds, at a true mean of -0.0149 and sd 0.0082:
   0.92, falling to 0.85 with one seed lost and 0.72 with two.

Date
----
31 October 2026, after a trial run on development seed 100 and before any run on seeds 200 to
207. Everything on seeds 0 to 9 and the recipe tuning on seeds 100 and 101 had been seen. The
analysis was to run once, after every run had finished, and only after the design of the
MultiEURLEX repair and the inclusion rules of a DistilBERT repair had been fixed in dated plans.

Departures
----------
- Head repair was dropped from this experiment on 31 October 2026, after the trial run and
  before any run on new seeds, because a check of its cached-feature path failed there. The
  head-repair result of the shallower repairs stands on seeds 0 to 9 only.
- Inferiority here requires the test beyond the margin, where the shallower repairs' second
  reading required only a material negative difference.
- A DistilBERT version of this experiment on the vocabulary stream, planned on 1 November 2026,
  was not run to completion.
