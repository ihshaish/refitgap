Analysis plan: the vocabulary stream
====================================

Aim
---
The five-corpus result is observational. If the association between vocabulary and labels is
moved deliberately on the ASRS stream, which is otherwise stable and shows no ranking loss, this
plan tests whether ranking quality falls, and whether only when the embeddings are tuned.

Design
------
From the second period onward, occurrences of the 1697 most label-discriminative tokens are
permuted among themselves with probability rho, drawn again each period. Labels, label priors
and corpus size are fixed, and the marginal token distribution is almost unchanged. Association
stability measured before training:
  primary stream (control)      rho 0      0.904   (0% of tokens below 0.5)
  paired swap                   rho 0.50   0.844   (3%)
  permutation                   rho 0.50   0.818   (6%)
  permutation                   rho 1.00   0.594   (30%)
The full permutation reaches the stability of the arXiv cs subcategories stream (0.595), which
lost only calibration with frozen embeddings and lost ranking with tuned ones.
BiGRU with frozen GloVe and with tuned GloVe embeddings, sequential training, 3 seeds per cell.

Measures
--------
Mean backward transfer in macro-AUPRC, per stream and embedding condition.

Predictions
-----------
1. Monotonicity: mean macro-AUPRC backward transfer declines monotonically in the strength of
   the manipulation, in both conditions.
2. Crossing: the full permutation shows negative macro-AUPRC backward transfer with tuned
   embeddings, of the order of the arXiv cs subcategories result (about -0.013).
3. The split by condition reproduces: with frozen embeddings the full permutation stays
   non-negative or close to zero.
The mechanism fails if macro-AUPRC backward transfer is unchanged across the doses, if
forgetting is as strong at rho = 0.5 as at rho = 1.0, or if the frozen condition degrades as
much as the tuned one.

Date
----
8 August 2026, before any model was trained on these streams.

Departures
----------
None.
