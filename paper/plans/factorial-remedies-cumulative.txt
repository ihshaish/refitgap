Analysis plan: both drifts crossed, by remedy
=============================================

Aim
---
This plan tests whether the remedy that works depends on the cause of the deficit: a threshold
refit enough under prior reweighting and not under a vocabulary change, both parts present when
both causes act. It also compares a model retrained on all accumulated data with sequential
training.

Design
------
BiGRU on frozen GloVe embeddings, ASRS streams. The two extreme earlier streams: the full
prior reweighting (control: its uniform subsample) and the full vocabulary permutation (control: the
primary stream). Two-by-two design on the 35% subsample: neither cause; reweighting only;
vocabulary only (the paired token swap at rho = 0.5 applied to the control's records); both.
Remedies, each applied to the final sequential model of the stream and of its matched control,
and scored on the untouched test set of each period. The calibration sample is 500 records from
the period's training pool, 3 independent draws per seed and period, the same records for every
remedy:
  inherited thresholds theta1 (reference);
  refit: thresholds refitted on the sample, model untouched;
  head adaptation: the head trained on the sample, then the refit (Adam, 3e-3, 3 epochs);
  full adaptation: all trainable parameters trained on the sample, then the refit (Adam, 3e-4, 5
    epochs).
Fitting thresholds on the adaptation sample slightly favours the two adaptations. Their settings
were chosen on two development streams outside the analysed cells (the 40% prior dose and a
quarter-dose swap), by mean recovery, on 26 August 2026 before the first run on an analysed cell.
A fine-tune whose macro-F1 on its own sample falls more than 0.05 below the value before
adaptation is marked diverged and reported as it is.
Comparators outside this sequence: an oracle prior-odds correction of the scores with the true
training priors (prior-bearing cells only; not a deployable remedy), and replay with a 5%
reservoir trained on the stream (descriptive).
Seeds: 10 per primary cell (both extreme streams, both controls, all four cells of the design);
replay at 5; the cumulative baseline at 3.

Analysis
--------
For a stream a, its control c and a remedy r, with Y the macro-F1 achieved:
  deficit under r: d(a,r) = Y(c,r) - Y(a,r);
  recovery: G(a,r) = d(a, inherited) - d(a,r);
  G*(a,r): the same on the attainable level (exact split-half, 200 partitions);
  repaired fraction of the refit: psi(a) = G(a, refit) / d(a, inherited).
Every planned quantity has a fixed reference; none uses backward transfer or a fraction of
headroom. Bootstrap over seeds, 20000 resamples, keeping all remedy observations and draws of a
seed together and resampling stream and control seeds independently. Intervals are given on the
planned contrasts; there is no significance threshold, and a miss is reported as a miss.
Precision: at 10 seeds a difference-in-differences has a standard error of about 0.004, so the
smallest meaningful recovery is 0.008 macro-F1, and a ratio whose denominator is below 0.010 or
not positive is marked as not evaluable.
Manipulation checks before any training on the new streams: prior drift of at least 0.12 in the
reweighting cells; the vocabulary change moves a cell's priors by less than 0.01; and the two
criteria given under Departures.

Predictions
-----------
1. Known from the earlier streams and not a prediction: the recoverable fraction separates the
   two causes, 97% [89, 104] against 9 to 16%.
2. Primary: psi(prior) > 2 x psi(permutation). The twofold margin was chosen after the earlier
   results and before this design.
3. Primary: G*(permutation, full adaptation) - G*(prior, full adaptation) > 0, with a bootstrap
   interval excluding zero. Secondary, direction only: the same order for head adaptation and
   for replay.
4. Primary: G(prior, refit) / G(prior, full adaptation) >= 0.8 and G(permutation, refit) /
   G(permutation, full adaptation) < 0.5, both denominators at or above 0.010. The cuts are
   practical, not significance thresholds.
5. Direction only: G(prior, oracle correction) < G(prior, refit).
6. In the cell with both causes, both parts of the deficit against the cell with neither are at
   least 0.008: the mismatch part and the part unavailable to threshold choice. Exploratory: the
   interaction of each part across the four cells, with intervals; non-additivity is a finding.
7. Descriptive, no direction predicted: the final model retrained from a new initialisation on
   all accumulated data each period (3 epochs, same architecture and optimiser, not matched in
   compute) against the final sequential model on the same historical periods: differences in
   macro-F1 at each model's own first-period thresholds, in macro-AUPRC and in the attainable
   level.
If 2 or 4 fails, the paper does not say which remedy a cause calls for. If 3 fails, that reading
is weakened. Head adaptation failing where full adaptation succeeds is not a failure: it places
the recoverable loss deeper than the head.

Date
----
25 August 2026 (second version), before any run of this design; no outcome of any planned cell
had been seen. The earlier result of prediction 1 and part of the refit's behaviour under
reweighting were already known. Data collection closed on 27 August 2026.

Departures
----------
Two manipulation criteria were corrected on 26 August 2026, after the check statistics were
computed and before any training on the new cells: control drift within 0.02 of the primary
stream's natural drift (0.0602), in place of at most 0.03; and a stability ratio of the
vocabulary cell to its counterpart within [0.85, 0.995], in place of below 0.70.
The cumulative baseline refitted its thresholds every period, so the first comparison credited
it with a refit. The comparison is reported with both models at first-period thresholds, and the
baseline's own refit separately.
On 23 October 2026 the interaction terms of prediction 6 were recomputed under one definition
of the control cell, which the first table had set to zero.
