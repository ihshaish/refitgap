Study design
============

Aim
---
Benchmarks in continual learning construct their drift; in three decades of ASRS aviation
safety reports the drift is measured. Three aims: (1) to locate the forgetting of a recurrent
text classifier under temporal drift, in the embedding layer or the decision layers, and to test
whether frozen corpus embeddings stabilise it; (2) to predict forgetting per period from
unlabelled statistics of the token distribution, computed from past data only; (3) to test
whether a replay buffer chosen to cover decaying vocabulary beats uniform reservoir replay at the
same memory budget.

Design
------
Streams. ASRS yearly exports. A corpus check comes first: record counts, casing, label priors,
year-by-year divergence of token distributions, breaks. Near-duplicates are removed (MinHash and
Jaccard over token shingles) before the split. Main stream 2010 to 2021; a second stream from
the earlier years (first set as 1988 to 2008, then 1999 to 2008 after the corpus check; 1988 to
1998 for text only), never pooled with the first. 2009 is left out of both and serves as a
positive control: the known break must show in the drift measures. Each stream has 8 contiguous
periods of equal record count; a division into 6, and boundaries shifted by half a period, are
sensitivity checks.
Task. Multi-label classification over the 14 top-level Anomaly categories, macro-F1 with
macro-AUPRC beside it. A label enters only if every period's test set has at least 75 positives.
Splits. A held-out test set per period, of fixed size (for example 2000 records), drawn once
under one global data seed, never trained on and identical for every condition and seed. The
training pool per period is cut to one size. Tokenisation uses no future period and no test set.
Learner. BiGRU, sequential training. Six embedding conditions: frozen corpus embeddings
(Avi2Vec), frozen GloVe 200d, tuned from each of these, random and trainable, random and frozen.
A model state is saved after each period.
Inherited thresholds. One threshold per label is fitted once on the first-period validation split
and kept for the whole stream. Recalibrating the thresholds alone is the lowest of three
repairs, before refitting the head and restoring the embedding.
Replay study. Buffers by vocabulary decay, by the same coverage with static weights, and
reservoir, at 0.5%, 1%, 2% and 5% of the stream; other selection baselines and EWC; a small
DistilBERT tier; one public chronological text task (HuffPost).

Analysis
--------
The full matrix of macro-F1 on period j's test set after training through period i, for every
run; final accuracy, backward transfer, and forward transfer relative to a per-period model
trained from scratch. Ten seeds for every contrast that carries a p-value; five-seed cells are
descriptive. Main inference: a mixed-effects model over per-period forgetting (condition fixed;
seed and period random). Paired permutation tests on ten-seed contrasts are a check, with Holm
within each contrast family and paired mean differences with intervals. For question 2: a
mixed-effects regression of forgetting on the drift measure (effective n = 8, said plainly),
with the period that spans COVID left out in turn. Method settings are tuned only on a
validation slice of the first two periods and then kept.

Predictions
-----------
1. Backward transfer degrades in the order frozen corpus embeddings, frozen GloVe, tuned, random
   and trainable (least forgetting first), with the frozen corpus embeddings' final accuracy
   statistically indistinguishable.
2. Freezing any well-covering embedding reduces forgetting; the corpus embeddings do so at
   less cost in plasticity than the generic one. On stability at equal performance the order is
   frozen domain, frozen GloVe, frozen random.
3. With trainable embeddings, refitting only the head on the final features recovers a minority
   of the lost per-period accuracy, and restoring the earlier embedding a majority. This runs
   against the usual finding; either outcome is informative.
4. Forgetting per period correlates positively, across periods and seeds, with the divergence
   between that period's token distribution and later periods', and with the share of its
   token mass on tokens that later decay.
5. At equal buffer size, replay by vocabulary decay is no worse than reservoir replay in
   backward transfer and accuracy, the advantage widening as the buffer shrinks and vanishing
   at generous sizes.
6. Records the sequential learner forgets are richer in decayed vocabulary than records it
   retains (odds ratio above 1).
7. Under frozen corpus embeddings, the advantage of prediction 5 shrinks but survives at
   the smallest buffer.
Fallback: if forgetting is mild we escalate (16 shorter periods, a smaller training budget per
period, or the hardest stable task); if it still stays within noise, the paper reports a
negative result on when forgetting bites, decided by the end of September.

Date
----
2 Aug 2026, before any neural run, with the binding changes of that day (inference with ten
seeds, tuning protocol, thresholds fitted once, the 75-positives rule, the frozen random
condition, near-duplicate removal).

Departures
----------
3 Aug 2026. Test sets are re-encoded with the current vocabulary before every evaluation; every
    run was repeated and no earlier row is analysed.
3 Aug 2026. The first runs showed no forgetting for replay to repair; the replay study was
    dropped, as the plan specified for that case.
8 Aug 2026. Split-half estimator: thresholds of the attainable level are fitted on one half of a
    period's test records and scored on the other, not on the labels they are scored against
    (fitting in-sample is optimistic by about 0.011 macro-F1). Conditions share seeds and test
    records and are averaged within seed first, giving ten blocks.
9 Aug 2026. HuffPost's five categories are fixed by schema stability, not by the 75-positives
    rule; three of them fall below it.
20 Sep 2026. The inherited thresholds are those the sequential run deployed, fitted from the
    first-period model's scores on the first-period validation split. Every printed result was
    regenerated on them; thresholds from the final model's scores are a sensitivity check.
