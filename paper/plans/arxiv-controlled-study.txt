arXiv controlled study: stopped at its design criterion
=======================================================

We fixed this plan on 28 Aug 2026.

Plan
    An external test of the pattern seen on ASRS. On arXiv, a reweighted stream at the strongest
    dose that met the plan's support constraints (about half the ASRS displacement), then
    sequential training on 20 seed blocks with three branches each, read against the ASRS
    predictions.

Criterion
    Before any final block was trained, five design-only scorers (same recipe, disjoint seeds)
    had to show a mean refit gain of at least 0.010 macro-F1: what a fixed learner recovers by
    refitting its thresholds on the shifted periods. Below that, the plan said, the effect is
    too small to estimate and the study stops.

Value
    +0.0087 (the five scorers: +0.0104, +0.0064, +0.0121, +0.0073, +0.0074), against the
    required 0.010. Not met, so the study was not run.

Departures
    A first execution of the criterion had joined its evaluation weights to a differently
    ordered copy of the pool; we corrected it before any final training, with the required
    0.010 unchanged. The stop says nothing about transfer, in either direction: the final runs
    never happened.
