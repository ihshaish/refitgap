Analysis plan: the reweighted stream
====================================

Aim
---
This plan tests two expectations for a stream whose label priors alone move: the model keeps its
ranking quality while its thresholded performance falls, and a threshold refit recovers the
loss here but not under the vocabulary permutation.

Design
------
Records are reweighted, never edited: no text and no label changes, only the mixture of records
per period, so P(x | y) is preserved and the manipulation is pure label shift. The vocabulary
permutation is the mirror image: it removes class associations and leaves priors untouched.
Three streams, each a subsample at keep = 0.35 without replacement: a uniform control of the
same size, a 40% dose and a full dose. Each period is split at random into two disjoint halves
and the tilt is applied within each; one half supplies training records, the other the test set,
so training and test priors move together.
                   train   test   train prior drift   test prior drift   min test positives
  control          14623  14623        0.067               0.065                 86
  40% dose        14623  14623        0.109               0.114                 83
  full dose        14623  14623        0.164               0.155                 99
(drift: mean absolute change in label priors, first against final period; positives per label
per period). No record has both roles within a period, and every label has at least 75 test
positives.
Manipulation check, run before this plan was written: the stability of P(token | label), which
label shift leaves unchanged by construction, calibrated against each stream's permutation null,
is 0.787, 0.724 and 0.669 across the three streams. The residual drift of 0.118 is reported as a
limit beside every result from these streams. The vocabulary permutation moves the same
statistic from 0.875 to 0.008.

Measures
--------
Mean backward transfer in macro-AUPRC and in macro-F1, BiGRU on frozen GloVe embeddings,
sequential training, 5 seeds; and the share of the macro-F1 deficit recovered by refitting the
one threshold per label on 500 labelled records per period.

Predictions
-----------
1. Full-dose macro-AUPRC backward transfer is at least the control's minus 0.005: ranking
   quality is kept.
2. Full-dose macro-F1 backward transfer is below the control's minus 0.005: thresholded
   performance falls, because the F1-optimal operating point depends on prevalence.
3. Dose-response: macro-F1 backward transfer is ordered control > 40% dose > full dose (1/6 by
   chance).
4. Refitting thresholds on 500 labelled records per period recovers at least 50% of the
   macro-F1 deficit on the full-dose stream and less than 20% on the vocabulary permutation
   stream. This contrast is the result; predictions 1 to 3 alone fit weaker accounts.
The account fails if: the full-dose macro-AUPRC backward transfer is clearly below the control's
(by more than 0.01); the refit fails on the reweighted deficit or succeeds on the permutation
deficit; macro-F1 shows no dose-response despite a measured prior movement; or the manipulation
check drifts further, once models are trained, than the 0.064 recorded under the first build.
All streams are reported whatever they show.

Date
----
Before any model was trained on the rebuilt streams; the construction was rebuilt on 8 August
2026.

Departures
----------
The first build resampled the training records only (23394 per stream) and left the test sets at
the original priors, which is not label shift. It was replaced by the build above before the
analysed runs. One stream (the 40% dose) had been trained under the first build; those results
are discarded and not reported. The residual drift is larger under the rebuild (0.118 against
0.064), because the disjoint halves leave fewer records per label.
