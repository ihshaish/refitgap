Method comparison: the part each method reduces
===============================================

Aim
---
On two ASRS streams whose cause of drift is fixed by construction, this plan tests whether each
continual-learning method reduces a different part of the deficit, the attainable gap or the
mismatch.
Under sequential training the deficit splits as 0.001 + 0.036 (attainable gap + mismatch) on the
reweighted stream and as 0.194 + 0.025 on the vocabulary stream.

Design
------
Two pairs, each an intervention stream and its control: the reweighted stream against a uniform
subsample, and the fully permuted vocabulary stream against the unmodified ASRS stream. Nine
labels, eight periods. Learner: BiGRU with frozen GloVe embeddings, 3 epochs per period.
Five arms, each applied from the first period: sequential training; replay (a 5% reservoir, not
tuned); EWC (per-example empirical diagonal Fisher); LwF (binary distillation, T = 2); and extra
updates on current data, a control that takes replay's number of optimiser steps but draws the
added records from the current period. One implementation per method family.
Seeds 0 to 9, the same seed for all five arms on all four streams: 200 runs, ten pairs per
contrast, no seed selection, no interim look and no extension.
EWC's lambda and LwF's alpha were chosen per intervention stream on two separate seeds (100, 101),
on 5% of each period's training pool held out from training: the setting with the highest mean
macro-F1 at the inherited thresholds over all periods seen at each checkpoint, ties (equal at 3
decimals) to the weaker setting, one grid extension if an endpoint is chosen. No test score was
read. Chosen: EWC lambda 0.001 on the reweighted stream (the weakest value, tied with sequential
training) and 1 on the vocabulary stream; LwF alpha 0.1 on both. Each control takes its
intervention stream's setting.

Analysis
--------
Per seed and pair, from the final models over all eight periods: deficit D = control minus
intervention in macro-F1 at thresholds refitted from the final model's own scores on the
first-period validation split; attainable gap A, the same difference in split-half attainable
level (200 partitions shared by all arms); mismatch R = D - A. A contrast is sequential training
minus the method, paired by seed (Delta A, Delta R; positive where the method reduced that part).
Delta A_0 is the attainable contrast in a common environment: both final models scored on the
control stream's test records, without any record of the paired stream's training pool, over
periods 4 to 8 on the reweighted pair and 3 to 8 on the vocabulary pair. Each contrast: mean, 95%
paired t interval (9 df), TOST at a margin of 0.005, and Holm within each arm's family of four
(Delta A_0 and Delta R on each pair), separately for the TOST and the t-test. Outcomes: equivalent
(TOST), material (non-zero and at least 0.005), small non-zero, unresolved. An exact sign-flip
test (1024 patterns) is the check: a non-zero outcome it does not support at
0.05 is read as unresolved.
Between-method family (four contrasts, Holm across them): LwF minus replay in Delta A_0 on each pair
(two-sided, reported), in net Delta A on the vocabulary stream (planned material and negative) and
in Delta R on the reweighted stream (must not be material and negative). Also given on seeds 5 to
9. Replay minus extra updates on current data (two contrasts, Holm across them): Delta R on the
reweighted stream and net Delta A on the vocabulary stream. Replay's reduction is put down to
stored history only if the contrast is material and positive; otherwise it reads "not separated
from extra updates on current data". Learning rule: if a method's attainable level at first
learning falls on a control stream (interval below -0.005), its statements are withheld. If it
falls on the intervention stream, a favourable outcome reads "preserves by adapting less" and an
equivalence is not counted. The summary sentence (LwF reduces the mismatch, replay the attainable
gap) is allowed only if the between-method family, the learning rule and both
replay-minus-extra-updates contrasts hold. The predictions explain that outcome; they do not
decide it. Reported without adjustment, deciding nothing: the aggregate Delta D, the net Delta A,
periods 2 to 7, the inherited thresholds, the historical periods (1 to 3 reweighted, 1 to 2
vocabulary), the other crossed terms, the split by time of learning, other threshold policies and
the unmodified stream. Power, from the sequential runs: equivalence on Delta A_0 is weak at ten
pairs (0.67 on the reweighted pair, 0.06 on the vocabulary pair, before Holm), and equivalence on
Delta R cannot be shown; an effect of 0.02 is detected with power 0.99.

Predictions
-----------
1. LwF's Delta R on the reweighted stream is material and positive. Fails if equivalent, or material
   and negative.
2. LwF's Delta A_0 on the vocabulary pair (periods 3 to 8) is equivalent. Fails if material, either
   sign. Not counted if LwF's learning is restricted.
3. Replay's Delta R on the reweighted stream is material and positive. Fails as in 1. Already seen
   on five seeds (+0.019), so this is a repeat.
4. Replay's Delta A_0 on the vocabulary pair (periods 3 to 8) is material and positive. Fails as in
   1. Already seen unpaired (+0.089) and on five seeds (+0.080 in net Delta A), so this is a repeat.
5. EWC's two Delta A_0 contrasts are equivalent; its two Delta R contrasts are reported with
   intervals and outcomes only, since equivalence on Delta R cannot be decided at ten pairs. A
   stream counts only where EWC's learning is not restricted. Holds when both streams count and
   both are equivalent; fails if either counted Delta A_0 is material.
No prediction on the net Delta A of either pair or on Delta R on the vocabulary stream. The whole
reading fails if prediction 2 fails or LwF shows replay's pattern (its Delta A_0 on the vocabulary
pair and its Delta R on the reweighted stream both material and positive). On MultiEURLEX (added
27 Oct 2026, before its runs and before any result of the main comparison was read): sequential
training's mismatch minus LwF's, periods 2 to 7 at the inherited thresholds, ten seeds, is
material and positive. One contrast, no Holm; LwF at alpha 0.1, T = 2, not re-tuned.
