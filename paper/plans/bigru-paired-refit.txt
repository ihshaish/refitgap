Analysis plan: replay against sequential training after refit (BiGRU, paired)
=============================================================================

Aim
---
To measure how much of replay's apparent retention advantage over sequential training remains
when both models refit one threshold per label on equal terms.

Design
------
ASRS primary stream, 8 periods, unchanged. BiGRU on frozen GloVe embeddings, 3 epochs per
period, hyperparameters unchanged. Two arms: sequential training, and experience replay with a
reservoir buffer of 5% of the training pool replayed in full each period. Seeds 0 to 9, the
same seed for both members of a pair. The buffer is empty in the first period, so the two
members train the same first-period model and share the inherited thresholds theta1. Pairing rule:
the first-period scores and thresholds must be bit-identical within every pair; if any pair
fails, the design is reported as seed-matched rather than paired and the failure is stated.
All ten pairs are reported, with no selection among seeds. A run that fails to complete is rerun
with the same seed, and a pair that cannot be completed is reported as missing. EWC and the
history windows are not rerun.

Measures
--------
For each pair and each historical period 2 to 7 (fixed here, before the runs), on the final
model:
  U_m   macro-F1 of arm m at the inherited thresholds theta1;
  V_m   attainable macro-F1 after refit, by exact split-half selection over 200 partitions
        shared between the two members of a pair;
  R_m = V_m - U_m, the mismatch;
  G_U = U_replay - U_seq, replay's lead under inherited thresholds;
  G_V = V_replay - V_seq, replay's lead after both refit with the same calibration budget;
  Delta = G_U - G_V = R_seq - R_replay, the part of the lead that is mismatch.
Each is averaged over periods within a pair, then over the 10 pairs.

Tests
-----
Primary: Delta, mean over the 10 pairs, with a percentile bootstrap interval over pairs (20000
draws), the count of pairs with Delta > 0 and the exact two-sided sign-flip p (minimum 2/1024 at 10
pairs). The intended test is the bootstrap interval.
Secondary:
1. G_V with its interval, read against a margin of 0.005 macro-F1 fixed here (under a third of
   replay's earlier gain and below the between-run standard deviation of backward transfer,
   0.004 to 0.008). "Replay retains an advantage after refit" requires the interval of G_V to
   lie above 0.005; "no material advantage" requires it to lie below 0.005; anything else is
   unresolved.
2. The refit from 500 and from 200 labelled records drawn from the period's test set (20 draws),
   evaluated on the remaining test records for both members: G at n = 500 and 200.
3. Current-period performance: the period-j model on period j, for j = 1..7 as the plan indexed
   them (the historical range above is j = 1..6), under theta1 and under the split-half refit,
   replay minus sequential.
4. Resources: the labelled historical records replay keeps (5% of the training pool) against
   the calibration records the refit uses per period (about half a test period, or 500 or 200).

Readings
--------
1. Delta > 0 with an interval excluding zero and G_V above the margin: replay preserves performance
   beyond what threshold choice recovers, and conventional evaluation also credits it with a
   decision-rule benefit.
2. Delta > 0 with an interval excluding zero and G_V below the margin: conventional backward
   evaluation attributes to replay a benefit that a threshold refit on the sequential model
   obtains without stored data; the method ranking is a property of the threshold policy.
3. The interval of Delta includes zero: the earlier composition is not reproduced; we report the
   paired estimates and say nothing about the policy dependence of the ranking.
4. G_V unresolved against the margin: reported as unresolved at 10 pairs, with the margin.

Date
----
22 October 2026, before any run of this design. An earlier unpaired grid (5 replay runs against
3 sequential runs; replay's gain 0.018, of which 0.016 was a reduction in mismatch) informed
the hypothesis only and stays in the paper as the unpaired pilot.

Departures
----------
None.
