Analysis plan: full-model repair of the final sequential DistilBERT on MultiEURLEX
=================================================================================

Aim
---
On MultiEURLEX, this plan tests whether full-model repair of the final sequential DistilBERT,
fitted on the final replay buffer at the end of the stream, matches replay on historical and
current performance within 0.005 under the shared rule, with no more training than replay's
extra updates. The
result is one row beside the BiGRU repair on MultiEURLEX. It is not a timing study, not a
head-only repair and not a buffer-only deployment.

Design
------
- Runs: the six paired-comparison runs on MultiEURLEX (seeds 0 to 5; sequential training, replay
  and extra updates on current data), reused without new training. Periods are numbered 0 to 7.
- Repair: every parameter of the final sequential model, fitted on the final buffer of the same
  seed's replay run (1801 records, 193 to 280 per period). AdamW with weight decay 0.01, batch
  32, constant learning rate, optional penalty towards the starting point, no evaluation data,
  no early stopping. One repair per seed.
- Recipe: chosen on two development initialisations (seeds 300 and 301, sequential and replay
  runs that withhold 10% of each period's training records). Grid: learning rate {1e-5, 2e-5,
  5e-5} x penalty {0, 1} x epochs {1, 3, 6, 10, 20}. Twenty epochs are 36020 record passes,
  under replay's 37821 extra ones.
- Selection rule, copied from the BiGRU repair on MultiEURLEX: highest held-out historical
  score, subject to a held-out current score at least that of the threshold refit minus 0.0025,
  means over the two development seeds; ties at three decimals go to fewer steps, then the
  smaller learning rate, then the larger penalty. Nothing from seeds 0 to 5 enters selection.
- Competence rule: the selected setting meets that constraint and scores above the threshold
  refit on held-out historical data on each development seed and on their mean. A setting on a
  grid edge is flagged and the grid is not widened. If the rule fails, no analysed seed is
  repaired and we report "no competent full repair found in the fixed search".

Analysis
--------
- Shared rule, as for the BiGRU: one threshold per label for each arm, fitted on the pooled
  fitting halves of the test sets of periods 0 to 7, macro-F1 on the held-out halves, mean over
  200 partitions and both directions. Historical is the mean over periods 0 to 6, current is
  period 7. The calibration records (about 9000) lie outside the buffer.
- Contrasts per seed: historical, repair minus replay (primary); current, repair minus replay;
  current, repair minus sequential, which tells whether the repair lowered its starting model;
  recovery, repair minus sequential on the historical periods.
- Margin 0.005 on the historical periods and on the current period, alpha 0.05, six paired
  initialisations; each contrast is a test family of one. Inferior and non-inferior each need
  the one-sided t test and the sign-flip test together, as in the BiGRU repair. With fewer than
  six passing seeds nothing is tested.
- Adequate: non-inferior on the three contrasts (intersection-union), and the cost condition on
  training passes: the repair processes fewer records than replay's extra ones (37821) in every
  initialisation. The cost of the recipe search is reported beside it, outside the condition.
- Descriptive: the gap to close (replay minus sequential, historical), the recovered share, the
  costs, and dating a lead: replay's lead when each period was first learned and at the
  endpoint, for replay against sequential training and against extra updates on current data.

Predictions
-----------
Each contrast is reported separately, historical before current, as non-inferior, below beyond
0.005 or unresolved. The combined readings are those of the BiGRU repair:
1. Adequate: performance adequate and the cost condition met.
2. Inferior: historical performance below replay by more than 0.005.
3. Trade-off: current performance below replay or below sequential by more than 0.005. "Replay's
   current-period advantage persists" is written when the repair is below replay but not below
   its starting model; "recovery-adaptation trade-off" only when historical performance is
   within 0.005 of replay and current performance fell more than 0.005 below sequential.
4. Not decided: everything else, printed with the estimates.
A shortfall is described for the selected recipe only, never as "repair fails" in general or
"earlier replay is necessary". No outcome shows whether replay's timing caused a difference.
The half-widths of the BiGRU repair at six seeds (historical 0.0021, current 0.0047) are a
guide to precision only.

Date
----
13 November 2026, before any development or repair run had been trained. The selected recipe
was added on 14 November 2026, after the development runs and before any repair outcome on the
analysed initialisations: learning rate 1e-5, penalty 1, 3 epochs (171 steps). It met the
competence rule (held-out historical 0.7686 against 0.7544 on the development mean).

Departures
----------
- The repair was designed after the six paired-comparison runs it reuses had been analysed. The
  runs give no independent test of a difference between BiGRU and DistilBERT.
- The selected learning rate is the lowest of the grid. Every setting without the penalty failed
  the current-score constraint.
