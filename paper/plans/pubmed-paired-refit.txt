Analysis plan: four arms on a PubMed stream after refit (DistilBERT, paired)
============================================================================
Fixed by Hisham: 2026-11-16
Fixed before the development stage and before any model was scored on the stream. The LwF setting
chosen in development is recorded in a separate dated note (LWF_SELECTION.txt) before any
confirmatory run. The run manifest records the hashes of code, data and plan.

Aim
---
To measure on a third stream how much of each method's historical lead remains once both models
refit one threshold per label, and whether the refit moves LwF's position relative to replay. No
prediction about direction or size. Completed results are reported whatever they show. No stream,
period, label or initialisation is added in response to the results.

Stream
------
NLM PubMed 2026 baseline with MeSH 2026; publication years 1995 to 2010; manually indexed MEDLINE
records; journal articles in English with an abstract of at least 50 words. Ordered by publication
month: the PubDate year with the PubDate month, or else the Entrez month when its year equals the
PubDate year, or else June; within a month by ascending PMID. A sample of every fifth
baseline file at a draw rate of 0.0609: 76080 records in eight periods of about 9510. Each period
has 7607 to 7609 records in its training pool and 1901 to 1902 test records. The 760 validation
records of the first period are set aside from its training pool. No validation or test record
enters training, replay or the control. Periods are cut by record count, so a boundary month can
fall in two adjacent periods.
Labels: the 13 top-level MeSH categories (A C D E F G H I J L M N Z) that meet the paper's support
criterion in every period; 4.86 labels per record. The two rarest labels have 58 and 60 positives
in the first-period validation split; this is reported, and the label set and threshold grid stay
as they are. Text: title and abstract, first 256 subword tokens; 69% of records are truncated.
Fixed by hash: the source manifest, the MeSH mapping and the stream file, which holds the record
order (ties included), the period boundaries and the split of every record
(sha256 657dacdce5bdf795b8cfa67452fb2c98a6960cacda1bbcfdf7fc8f3ba6d5e2d3).
The task is the classification of historically dated records under the labels of one 2026
snapshot. Feasibility used data checks and a timing of GPU throughput; no model had been scored on
the stream when this plan was fixed.

Design
------
Learner and recipe as in distilbert-paired-refit.txt: distilbert-base-uncased, model and tokenizer
at one pinned revision; AdamW at 2e-5, linear warm-up over 10% of each period's updates, batch 32,
3 epochs per period. theta1 is fitted once on the first-period validation split on the paper's
19-point grid and then inherited.
Arms:
  sequential training;
  replay: a reservoir of 3043 records (5% of the training pool), replayed in full, not tuned;
  extra updates on current data: as many added records as replay takes from its reservoir, drawn
    from the current period's training records. Optimiser updates per period match replay's;
    both step counts and the numbers of current and earlier records processed are recorded. The
    control matches the number of updates, not total compute or the path of optimisation;
  LwF: binary distillation from the previous period's model on the current period's training
    records, T = 2. Loss: BCE + alpha T^2 BCEWithLogits(z/T, sigmoid(z_old/T)), with the BiGRU
    implementation's normalisation, checked term by term.
The first period is trained once per initialisation. All four arms continue from that one model
and its theta1; both hashes and the first-period scores are verified in every block.
LwF's alpha is chosen from 0.02, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2 on development initialisations 300 and 301, which
train with 5% of each period's training pool held out. Objective: macro-F1 at the inherited
thresholds on the held-out records; for each checkpoint the mean over the periods seen, then the
mean over checkpoints, then over the two initialisations. Ties (equal at 3 decimals) go to the
weaker setting. A chosen endpoint is reported; the grid is not extended. The selection reads the
held-out development files only and cannot open a confirmatory score. The other three arms run on
these two initialisations as a check of the pipeline at full size. The engineering checks under
Stops come before tuning.
Confirmatory runs: initialisations 0 to 7 for all four arms, 32 trajectories in eight blocks. No
selection among initialisations, no interim contrast, no extension. The selection note, with the
chosen alpha and the objective values, is fixed and hashed before the first confirmatory run.
Confirmatory outcomes are not computed or read until the collection is closed, by completion or by
a stop named below.

Analysis
--------
The unit is the initialisation. Periods and partitions are not further independent runs.
For the final model of arm m, per block and period 2 to 7:
  U_m: macro-F1 at theta1 on the whole period (the paper's convention);
  U_sf,m: macro-F1 at theta1 on the scoring halves used for V_m, averaged as V_m is;
  V_m: cross-fitted macro-F1 after refit. 200 seeded partitions shared by all arms of a block;
    thresholds fitted on one half and scored on the other, roles swapped, averaged over halves
    and partitions; the paper's conventions for the threshold grid, ties and labels without
    positives.
For arm a against arm b: G_U = U_a - U_b, G_V = V_a - V_b, and the change G_V - G_U. Periods are
averaged within a block, then blocks. The change is given with its 95% paired t interval and the
count of blocks by sign. Two leads falling in different classes do not show that a lead changed.
Comparisons: replay minus sequential training; LwF minus sequential training; replay minus extra
updates on current data. Each comparison is its own family {G_U, G_V}, analysed as in the
DistilBERT paired refit at the 0.005 margin. Equivalent: Holm-adjusted TOST within +/-0.005; this
class takes precedence. Otherwise non-zero by the Holm-adjusted paired t test: material if the
mean is at or beyond the margin, small non-zero if inside it, and in either case only if the exact
two-sided sign-flip test (unadjusted) also rejects zero. The sign-flip test does not alter an
equivalent outcome. All other leads are unresolved. No statement across the three comparisons
rests on these separate families.
One secondary contrast, on the common scoring halves, per block:
  D = (V_LwF - V_replay) - (U_sf,LwF - U_sf,replay),
the difference between the two changes against sequential training, whose terms cancel. Positive:
the refit moves the lead towards LwF relative to replay. Mean, two-sided 95% paired t interval,
number of blocks. An interval that spans zero is unresolved; it does not show equivalence.
Descriptive outputs, each on periods 2 to 7 unless named, none combined with another:
  periods 1 to 7, and period 8 with each arm's level;
  the inherited lead on the same folds for every comparison, beside the whole-period convention;
    if the two read differently, both are reported;
  a common cut of 0.5 in place of the inherited thresholds;
  the refit with a seeded subsample of 500 labelled records per fitting half, the same for all
    arms, scored on the unchanged other half;
  support and contribution of every label in the macro-average;
  each arm's level on a period at first learning and at the end, at theta1 on the same records,
    so that a lead present at first learning is told apart from a later change in the lead;
  the decomposition of backward transfer for the sequential arm, with the paper's reference and
    final checkpoints.
Scores per label are kept for every checkpoint on every period, with record identifiers, label
order, thresholds, arm, initialisation, period, split and the partition specification. Any
comparison with the dating analysis of Appendix F.4 uses one scoring convention throughout.

Stops and missing runs
----------------------
Before tuning, on the rebuilt bundle on Colab: the unit checks pass on the GPU; the compute cap is
shown to stop launches at a low test limit; one engineering run is interrupted on purpose and
recovered from the copied first-period model. In that exercise the restored model's hash, theta1,
the first-period scores and the schedule of updates (steps and record order) must agree exactly
with an uninterrupted reference run of the same development job. Later scores are compared and the
difference recorded; they are not
required to be identical, because training on this GPU is not bit-reproducible (identity of a
recovered run is shown on CPU). A failed check pauses the stage until resolved.
At most two technical restarts per trajectory (three starts), where the earlier DistilBERT plan
allowed one. Causes are failures of infrastructure (a lost session, memory), never a result or a
validation score. A restarted arm begins again from the same verified first-period model with the
same initialisation, bundle and data; its partial outputs are discarded; cause and cost are logged.
A job that fails on a live session is restarted by hand with its cause and counts as a restart.
No replacement initialisations.
Compute is capped at 80 Colab units for development, confirmation and any lost or repeated
sessions. At the cap the collection stops and is reported as incomplete; no top-up.
Each comparison uses its complete pairs, and D its complete LwF and replay pairs; the
initialisations that contribute are listed. A comparison with fewer than eight pairs is reported
with its estimates and intervals as descriptive, without outcome classes. A missing arm outside a
comparison does not affect it.
Before confirmation: the checks above pass; the analysis reproduces the three stored DistilBERT
comparisons (six pairs each, with their published classes); splits, labels and scores are aligned;
replay and the control match in updates on the
development runs; the launcher matches the fixed plan, the selection note, the stream and the code
bundle to their hashes. Step counts of each finished replay and control pair are verified before
it enters the analysis. No run is replaced or reconfigured after a confirmatory outcome is seen.

Limits stated in the article
----------------------------
The stream was added after the ASRS and MultiEURLEX results were known, with this plan fixed before
any model was scored on it. Labels come from one 2026 snapshot, not from the labels available at
each date. Thirteen broad categories, three with prevalence above 0.6; 69% of texts truncated. One
learner; LwF tuned in development, replay not tuned. The refit uses labels from the period
evaluated, also at 500 records. The shared first-period model differs from the earlier DistilBERT
protocol. The comparison is of recorded scores and decision rules; it identifies no mechanism of
forgetting.
