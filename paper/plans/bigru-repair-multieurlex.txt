Analysis plan: full-model repair against replay on MultiEURLEX (BiGRU, new seeds)
=================================================================================

Aim
---
On natural temporal drift, this plan tests whether full-model repair of the final sequential
model, fitted on the final buffer of the paired replay run, falls more than 0.005 below replay
on historical performance under one shared rule. The test is whether a later repair reproduces
replay's endpoint advantage, not whether repair undoes forgetting: on seeds 0 to 9 sequential
training showed no average backward deficit on this stream, while replay led at the endpoint by
+0.0273 (sd 0.0040).

Design
------
- Stream: MultiEURLEX, 45028 records in 8 periods by date (1988 to 2014), numbered 0 to 7 (the
  paper numbers them from 1); 12 labels; the first 256 tokens of each act; no control stream.
  Learner: BiGRU with frozen GloVe embeddings, 3 epochs per period.
- Arms: sequential training, replay (5% reservoir, 1801 records) and extra updates on current
  data (as many extra current-period records, the same number of steps).
- Seeds 500 to 505 are analysed; 506 and 507 replace a seed only if a run check fails after
  its training started and one restart does not recover it, never because of an outcome.
  Development seeds 520 and 521 select the recipe and 522 is a trial run for timing. No seed is
  added after looking and there is no interim look.
- Repair: every trainable parameter of the final sequential model (embeddings frozen), fitted on
  the final buffer of the same seed's replay run; Adam, batch 64, no evaluation data. No head
  repair, no logit adapter, no union of buffers.
- Recipe: not carried over from ASRS. Fixed grid of 24 settings, learning rate {1e-4, 1e-3,
  1e-2} x penalty {0, 1} x epochs {1, 3, 10, 30}, on development seeds 520 and 521, which
  withhold 10% of each period's training pool. Selection as in the shallower repairs: highest
  held-out historical score, subject to a held-out current score at least that of the threshold
  refit minus 0.0025, means over the two seeds; ties at three decimals go to fewer steps, then
  the smaller learning rate, then the larger penalty.
- Competence rule: the selected setting meets that constraint and scores above the threshold
  refit on held-out historical data on each development seed and on their mean. A grid-edge
  setting is flagged and the grid is not widened. If the rule fails, no analysed seed runs and
  we report "no competent full repair found in the fixed 24-setting search".
- Go or stop, on development seeds only: runs complete and reproducible after an interruption;
  projected compute within 60 node-hours; sequential first-learning macro-F1 in every period no
  lower than the lowest value on seeds 0 to 9 minus 0.05; competence.

Analysis
--------
- Shared rule: one threshold per label for each arm, fitted on the pooled fitting halves of
  the test sets of periods 0 to 7, macro-F1 on the held-out halves, mean over 200 partitions and
  both directions. Historical is the mean over periods 0 to 6 with equal weights, current is
  period 7. The restricted range (periods 1 to 6) and any subset of periods are not used. The
  calibration records (about 9000) lie outside the buffer.
- Contrasts per seed: historical, repair minus replay (the only primary); current, repair minus
  sequential and repair minus replay; recovery, repair minus sequential on historical periods.
  Margin 0.005 on the historical periods and on the current period, alpha 0.05, at least 6
  passing seeds; each contrast is a test family of one, without Holm.
- Inferior: two-sided t and two-sided sign-flip tests below 0.05 with a mean of at least 0.005
  in size, and the one-sided t and one-sided sign-flip tests beyond -0.005 both below 0.05.
  Non-inferior: the one-sided t and sign-flip tests against -0.005 both below 0.05.
- Adequate: non-inferior on the historical and both current contrasts (intersection-union),
  and the cost condition on training passes: the repair processes fewer records than replay's
  extra ones in every seed. Replay's extra passes are 37821; the repair's are 1801 x epochs, so
  the condition can be met at 1, 3 or 10 epochs and not at 30.
- Descriptive: recovery as a share of replay's lead; replay minus sequential and replay minus
  extra updates on current data; values per period; each historical difference under per-period
  rules beside the shared rule; the costs.
- Dating a lead (acquisition): the lead of replay, and of extra updates on current data, over
  sequential on each period when it was first learned (a_j) and at the endpoint (e_j), their
  means over periods 0 to 6 (a_H, e_H) and the share a_H / e_H. A later repair cannot change
  a_j; it targets e_j, whatever its origin.

Predictions
-----------
1. Adequate: repair non-inferior to replay within 0.005 on historical and current performance.
2. Inferior: repair remained below replay by more than 0.005 on historical performance (the
   selected recipe only; an endpoint advantage, not reduced forgetting).
3. Trade-off, beside 2 or 4: current performance below sequential or replay by more than 0.005.
4. Not decided: neither 1 nor 2. No outcome is read as showing dependence on the corpus.
5. If repair recovers the same share as on the vocabulary stream (84%, historical about
   -0.0044), we expect reading 4 at six seeds. Inferiority becomes likely only if repair
   recovers less than about 63% of the gap at sd 0.006 (69% at 0.004, 52% at 0.009), and
   non-inferiority only at about full recovery. Planning sd 0.004 to 0.009.
6. "Not specific to the ASRS corpus and its constructed drift" is written only if this
   experiment and the vocabulary-stream repair are both inferior and recovery is positive here.
7. After reading 2, the remaining gap is called "in attainable performance" only if the
   difference under per-period rules is negative, its interval excludes zero and it is at least
   half the shared-rule difference in size.
8. If a_H / e_H is above one half, most of replay's lead was present at first learning; if the
   interval of replay minus extra updates on current data does not lie above zero, the lead is
   not shown to come from the retained records and not from the extra steps.

Date
----
1 November 2026, before any run of this experiment on seeds 500 to 529 and before any result of
the vocabulary-stream repair on new seeds was opened. Seeds 0 to 9 of this stream had been
analysed. The selected recipe was recorded on 2 November 2026, after the development seeds and
before the runs it governs: learning rate 1e-4, no penalty, 30 epochs (870 steps). It met the
competence rule (held-out historical 0.7292 against 0.7014 on seed 520, 0.7349 against 0.7104
on seed 521) and the decision was to go (projected 34.8 node-hours).

Departures
----------
- The selected recipe lies on two grid edges (lowest learning rate, most epochs).
- At 30 epochs the repair processes 54030 records against replay's 37821 extra ones, so the
  cost condition cannot be met; a repair adequate in performance then reads as not decided.
