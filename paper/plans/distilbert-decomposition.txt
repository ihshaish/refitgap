Analysis plan: the decomposition B = A + Delta R on DistilBERT
=========================================================

Aim
---
This plan tests whether the composition of the backward deficit found on the BiGRU, where the
change in mismatch Delta R is the larger part, extends to a transformer fine-tuned end to end.

Design
------
ASRS primary stream, unchanged. DistilBERT base uncased, fine-tuned end to end with sequential
training: AdamW at 2e-5, batch 32, 256 subword tokens, 3 epochs per period, settings unchanged.
Initialisations: 6 (seeds 0 to 5) if the GPU budget allows, else 5 (seeds 0 to 4); fixed at 6
before the first run. Kept for every run: the scores of every period model on every period's
test set, the inherited thresholds theta1 (fitted on the first-period validation split of the
first-period model) and every period model's scores on that validation split.

Analysis
--------
Macro-F1 at theta1; attainable levels by exact split-half selection over 200 partitions shared
between the two models; deficit B, attainable gap A and change in mismatch Delta R. Periods 2 to 7
are primary and the conventional range is reported beside them. Percentile bootstrap over
initialisations, 20000 resamples.
Primary quantity: Delta R - A on periods 2 to 7, mean over initialisations with its bootstrap
interval, and the count of initialisations with Delta R - A > 0. The intended test is the bootstrap
interval; the exact two-sided sign-flip p is reported beside it (minimum 2/64 = 0.031 with 6
initialisations, 0.0625 with 5). Reproducing the BiGRU share of 0.90 is not a criterion.
Secondary, descriptive: the share of the final model's mismatch recovered by a refit of the
final model's thresholds on first-period validation data; the refit from 500 and from 200
labelled records; the conventional-range values.

Readings
--------
1. Delta R - A > 0 with an interval excluding zero: the composition found on the BiGRU extends to an
   end-to-end transformer under this training regime.
2. Delta R - A < 0 with an interval excluding zero: the decomposition identifies a boundary of
   architecture or training regime, and the paper presents the ASRS composition as
   regime-sensitive.
3. The interval includes zero: the extension has limited precision at this number of seeds, and
   the paper says its strongest numerical statements remain specific to the BiGRU condition.
No stream, label set, period, threshold procedure or estimator changes. All runs are reported,
with no selection among seeds.

Date
----
13 October 2026, before any run of this design. Three published DistilBERT runs, which show
thresholded and threshold-free backward transfer diverging, and the BiGRU results informed the
hypothesis only.

Departures
----------
None.
