Threshold reporting in 34 studies
=================================

The 34 studies of the supplement's appendix on threshold reporting, and how each handles the rule
that turns scores into labels. Each entry comes from the full text, appendices included.

Policy labels used in the table:

- not stated: sigmoid or BCE multi-label head, F1 reported, and no sentence in the full text says how scores become labels. The full text was searched for "threshold"; the count is given where it is the basis of the label.
- 0.5 fixed, stated: the study states a fixed cut-off or an equivalent fixed comparison rule.
- tuned once, inherited: thresholds fitted on validation data once, then carried to later tasks.
- refit per task or period: thresholds refitted on each period's or task's validation data.
- argmax, implicit: single-label task; the decision rule is the argmax and is never discussed.
- generative exact match: the model emits the label string; scored by exact or set match; no score threshold exists.
- none: no thresholded metric reported.

1
    Study (authors, year, venue): Santosh, Vuong, Grabmair 2024, ChronosLex, ACL 2024
    Identifier: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14211
    Task: Multi-label text (6 legal datasets: EURLEX, UKLEX, ECHR and others)
    Metrics reported: micro-F1, macro-F1, mean R-Precision (m-RP)
    Thresholded metric: Yes (micro-F1, macro-F1)
    Threshold policy as stated: not stated ("threshold" 0 mentions in full text including
      appendices)
    Forgetting, BWT or backward decline on the thresholded metric: Yes. Chronological drop:
      train on periods up to t, test on t+1 (Eval-Fix) and streamed (Eval-Stream); no formal BWT
      formula
    Threshold-free metric beside it: Yes, m-RP (ranking)
    Quotation and location: Sec 6.1: "Following Chalkidis and Søgaard [2022], we report,
      micro-F1, macro-F1, and mean R-Precision (m-RP)." App. A: "For UKLEX and EURLEX, we employ
      the standard approach to compute macro-F1, micro-F1, and m-RP scores on the list of
      labels." App. A: "We fine-tune the models on our six datasets using binary cross-entropy
      loss". Sec 3: "we restrict the testing to the following time period t+1."

2
    Study (authors, year, venue): Wang, Li, Zhang, Chen 2025, CUTER, ICML 2025 (PMLR 267)
    Identifier: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19680
    Task: Multi-label image online CL (COCO, VOC, NUS-WIDE)
    Metrics reported: mAP, CF1, OF1 (cross-task average and final)
    Thresholded metric: Yes (CF1, OF1)
    Threshold policy as stated: not stated for the proposed method; 0.5 fixed, stated, for two
      prototypical baselines only
    Forgetting, BWT or backward decline on the thresholded metric: No formal forgetting metric;
      "Avg" and "Last" performance only
    Threshold-free metric beside it: Yes, mAP
    Quotation and location: Sec 3.1: "Following (Du et al., 2023; 2024), we use several standard
      metrics: mean average precision (mAP), per-class F1 score (CF1), and overall F1 score
      (OF1)." App. E.1: "For methods employing prototypical classifiers (iCarl and NsCE), we
      computed cosine similarities and applied a threshold of 0.5 to determine positive labels."

3
    Study (authors, year, venue): Zeng, Zhou, Zhan, Zhang 2026, MedCL-Bench, arXiv
    Identifier: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16738
    Task: Mixed biomedical NLP; multi-label topic classification (LitCovid) among 10 tasks;
      generative LLM
    Metrics reported: Accuracy (all tasks); subset accuracy for LitCovid; AP (average
      performance), BWT, FWT
    Thresholded metric: Yes (accuracy; exact-match subset accuracy)
    Threshold policy as stated: generative exact match; the model emits a ";"-separated label
      set; no score threshold exists
    Forgetting, BWT or backward decline on the thresholded metric: Yes. BWT and per-task
      forgetting Δ = a_t^post − a_t^end computed on subset accuracy
    Threshold-free metric beside it: No
    Quotation and location: Methods, Eval: "use accuracy as the primary metric, enabling
      consistent cross-task comparison." "For LitCovid (multi-label), we use subset accuracy
      (exact match)." Results: "forgetting on task t is defined as Δ = a_t^post − a_t^end
      (percentage points)".

4
    Study (authors, year, venue): Holzinger and Colletti 2026, Drift Happens (QCDS 2026
      extended)
    Identifier: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05908
    Task: Binary image (Yearbook), regression (Amazon), multi-label text (arXiv, 7 categories)
    Metrics reported: Accuracy (Yearbook), balanced MSE (Amazon), macro AUC (arXiv); temporal
      drift matrix; ID, Future and Decay scores
    Thresholded metric: Only for the binary image task (accuracy); none for the multi-label text
      task
    Threshold policy as stated: none for multi-label (AUC chosen because a single threshold "is
      arbitrary"); argmax or 0.5 implicit for Yearbook accuracy
    Forgetting, BWT or backward decline on the thresholded metric: Decay = ID − Future computed
      on each task's metric (accuracy, AUC, MSE); cross-period matrix M_ij
    Threshold-free metric beside it: Yes, macro AUROC (the only metric for the multi-label task)
    Quotation and location: App. B.3: "fixing a single decision threshold is arbitrary and tends
      to favour the frequent subjects." App. B.3: "We address the first by scoring each subject
      with the Area Under the ROC Curve." Sec 4.1: "The temporal drift matrix M∈ℝ^{K×K} captures
      cross-temporal generalization."

5
    Study (authors, year, venue): Chalkidis and Søgaard 2022, Findings of ACL 2022
    Identifier: https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07856
    Task: Multi-label text (UK-LEX, EUR-LEX, BIOASQ), chronological and random splits
    Metrics reported: micro-F1, macro-F1, m-RP
    Thresholded metric: Yes (micro-F1, macro-F1)
    Threshold policy as stated: not stated ("threshold" 0 mentions; head described as sigmoid
      per label)
    Forgetting, BWT or backward decline on the thresholded metric: Yes. Drop from random to
      chronological split on F1 ("demonstrates the temporal concept drift"); no BWT
    Threshold-free metric beside it: Yes, m-RP
    Quotation and location: Sec 5 "Evaluation Metrics": "retrieval measures have been favored in
      large-scale multi-label text classification literature … we also report the standard
      micro-F1 (µ-F1) and macro-F1 (m-F1)". Sec 4: "L linear layers (ol) with sigmoid
      activations … to produce the probability of the corresponding label pl". Fig. 1 caption:
      "The performance drop from random to chronological splits demonstrates the temporal
      concept drift."

14

      task.

Policy counts over the 34 studies, one primary thresholded metric per study. Not stated 15, fixed rule stated 1, tuned once then inherited 1, refit per task or period 0, argmax implicit 11, generative exact match 4, no thresholded metric 2.

Counting conventions. CUTER is counted once, under not stated; its 0.5 rule covers its iCaRL and NsCE baselines only. Agarwal and Nenkova is counted under not stated: the study reports F1 for one class and a macro-F1 without a stated rule.

Multi-label subset, 19 studies (rows 1 to 5, 11, 14, 23 to 34): 16 report a thresholded metric. Of those 16, 13 do not state the rule, 1 states a fixed rule (ML-BELS), 1 states tuned once then inherited (Ceccon), 1 is generative set match (MedCL-Bench). Five of the 19 are text studies (rows 1, 5, 11, 14, 33): four not stated, one threshold-free.

Decline on a thresholded metric with a fixed or unstated rule. A formal forgetting or BWT formula is applied to such a metric in 7 of 34 studies: AGCN, Ceccon, MedCL-Bench, TRACE, SAPT, Mehta, Ovcharov. Any cross-period or cross-task decline reported on such a metric: 20 of 34 studies; 7 of the 16 multi-label studies with a thresholded metric. Of those 20, one (Ceccon) states the policy.
