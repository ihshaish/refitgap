Changes
=======

0.2.0 (23 Nov 2026)
- evaluate(refit_n=...): a refit on a limited number of labelled records, on the same halves.
- evaluate(previous=...): the previous period's rule, scored on the same halves.
- evaluate(per_label=True) and Evaluation.label_summary(): per-label levels.
- rule_lead(): a method's lead under the previous period's rule (or any fixed rule) beside its
  lead after both refit; limited_draws() and previous_draws() expose the draws.

0.1.0 (15 Nov 2026)
- First release: compare(), method_contrast(), the evaluator and the command line.
